# Analysis of Trinidad & Tobago's Inflation (1991-2025)

This notebook covers Trinidad and Tobago's inflation, specifically the factors 
that may cause a rise in inflation, and the response that food inflation and 
core inflation may have to these factors.

## Data Source
Data sourced from the Central Bank of Trinidad and Tobago - available under the Prices tab.

https://www.central-bank.org.tt/statistics/data-centre/

## Key Findings
- T&T's high reliance on food imports has caused it to be vulnerable to food 
  price increases which stem from global shocks
- Food inflation in T&T is consistently higher than core inflation, which 
  supports the findings of the country's high reliance on food imports
- The five highest inflation years (2008, 1993, 2010, 2012, 1994) were each 
  driven by either global commodity shocks, domestic weather-related supply 
  disruptions, or currency devaluation — never by excess domestic spending
